“What a pity there’s no one to share this last and greatest moment with me.” Herzog made a great show of bowing in every direction. “Ladies and gentlemen, you are about to witness the coming of a new age! An age in which you… will be enslaved!”

He was too pleased with himself, too delighted, and the petty little man beneath it all showed himself completely. He scratched his ears and cheeks, waving his arms and stamping his feet like a monkey.

The transfusion tubes were already in the main arteries of Erii’s neck. Herzog inserted the two tubes into his own neck, and the blood-exchange machine began exchanging their blood. The newborn dragon’s blood flowed into Herzog’s body, while Herzog’s aging blood flowed into Erii’s. It was a great operation, unlike anything ever attempted before. Through the blood, the White King’s power entered Herzog’s body. His eyes grew brighter and brighter, as if lava were flowing in their depths. The same white threads began growing from his body. His skin slowly became smooth and supple, flushed pink like a baby’s. He spread his arms in pleasure and let the threads wrap around him, savoring the feeling of immense power flowing through his body.

No one spoke anymore. Only one sound echoed across the stage: the girl trapped inside the cocoon was quietly crying. She was calling someone’s name. She said… Sakura… Sakura… Sakura!

Lu Mingfei fell to his knees against that invisible wall. He felt like a dog with its backbone pulled out. At the very end, the very last moment, she was still calling his name.

A ridiculous made-up name.

He was the greatest hero in her life, but he had come too late.

When the crying finally stopped, a pure-white claw tore Herzog’s cocoon open from the inside. That perfect creature burst through the opening and spread his white, membranous wings in the air. He hovered in the well like a giant cross. The light reflected from his scales lit up the darkness.

His horns rose proudly from his head. He was graceful, elegant, somewhere between an angel and a devil. Even Xia Mi, when she became a dragon, had not been as perfect as he was. He was the new White King. The White King Herzog. A magnificent being beneath one alone and above all others. In an age without the Black King, the throne of the world was his!

A violent wind swept across the stage. Herzog shot upward, smashed through the kabuki theater’s roof, and vanished into the rainy sky.

“That’s why I said you were too late, Brother,” Lu Mingze said quietly.

No wonder he was dressed like that, without a smile on his face. Tonight, he really had come to a funeral.

Lu Mingfei stood at the very bottom of the Red Well. White threads surrounded him, like an enormous spider’s nest. There was rain above and rain below. Rain washed the blood from the ground. Not far away were two human figures holding each other tightly. Until the very last moment, Minamoto Chime had still been clinging to Minamoto Chisei. Perhaps he was afraid and wanted the warmth of his older brother. Or perhaps he wanted to keep his brother, trapped in a nightmare, from being afraid. Farther away, the girl’s body was faintly visible through an almost transparent cocoon.

He dragged his feet over and tore the white threads apart with his hands. He did not feel them eating into his skin. He dug the dried-up Erii out of the cocoon, took off his shiny little suit jacket, and wrapped it around her naked body.

He held her tightly. A long, long time later, he began to cry without a sound.

Lu Mingze had never taken him to the kabuki theater. That had only been an illusion. He had finally arrived at the Red Well, and in that imaginary theater he had watched the ending of this tragedy. He was too late. The real tragedy had already ended before he got there. He could change nothing.

“I still really want your soul piece, Brother, but I can’t change what’s already happened to exchange for it. My deals only work on the future. So please regret it. You've come too late.” Lu Mingze leaned against the wall of the well, arms folded, looking up into the rain. 

“This spring is about to be over. At the end of this season, the most wonderful thing in your life was supposed to happen to you. But you never took your chance.”

“Do you understand now? Without power and without strength, you can’t do anything at all! You should have been a monster roaring at the world. But no, you had chosen to tuck away your claws and fangs and be useless.”

“Born a monster, dying a good person. Or living like a good person, dying like a monster. Which ending is more tragic?”

Lu Mingze sounded as if he were casually talking about life with him.

Lu Mingfei turned Erii over gently. Between her sixth and seventh vertebrae, he found the scorpion-like parasite. Through her skin, it felt like a hard lump. In the end, it had chosen this place to attach itself, joining its nerve fibers to her spine and taking control of her body. Then it had injected the White King’s core genes into her.

Lu Mingfei picked up a discarded short knife and carefully cut into that spot. He wanted to dig out that piece of dried-up dragon bone. He couldn't allow this filthy thing left inside Erii.

Luckily, there was hardly any blood left in her body. When he cut through the skin and the pale muscle fibers, no blood came out. That made him feel a little better. But the Sacred Remains were joined so tightly to Erii’s spine that they had practically become one. He did not dare pull hard, as if he were afraid the girl could still feel pain. He could only use the knife to cut through those fine, tentacle-like bones, a little at a time.

At last he dug the Sacred Remains out. He hurled it to the ground, threw himself on it, and stabbed at it with the knife. But an ordinary knife was little use against dragon bone. Sparks flew from the tip.

Like a madman, he ran to get metal tools to smash it with. He burned it with a gas torch and sprayed it with liquid nitrogen. He spent every bit of strength in his body on that piece of dead bone.

Lu Mingze knew when to make himself useful. Hammers, pliers, gas torches—whatever Lu Mingfei wanted, he brought it over. When Lu Mingfei swung the hammer, he held the Sacred Remains steady with the pliers. When Lu Mingfei brought out the gas torch, he got the liquid-nitrogen nozzle ready. Hot, cold, hot, cold. They were going to finish the thing off.

They really did look like brothers then. One crazy enough, one ruthless enough, working together without a hitch. If the two of them teamed up to kill someone, it would be far too easy.

After they had tried every weapon in the arsenal, the Sacred Remains finally became a pile of white powder, with a few little burnt pieces mixed in. The great Sacred Remains had not moved once. The two brothers had tormented it to death.

Actually, it had been dead already. Many parasites were like that. Before finding a suitable host, they were full of life, moving vigorously about. Once they found one, they entered the reproductive stage, lost the ability to move, and slowly died. Now its genes had entered Herzog’s body in some form. Its work was done.

Lu Mingfei wished desperately that it would fight back a little. He wanted it to be like a little bug full of juice, something he could stamp on and burst with a pop. At least then there would have been a little satisfaction in his revenge. But the Sacred Remains gave him nothing. A dead pig wasn’t afraid of boiling water, after all.

He dropped the hammer, went back, and picked Erii up. He said nothing. He was thinking. Or perhaps his mind was empty.

“It’s too late to get vicious now. Half an hour earlier, you could have changed how this story ended. But what were you doing then? You were drinking. You were hesitating. You were telling yourself it was all right. By the time you made up your mind, it was too late.”

“How many times have I told you? Don’t let a chance you already have slip away. There aren’t many people in this world you like. There won’t be many who like you, either.”

“All right, keep that quarter of your life. I can’t have it, but you can’t trade it to get that girl back, either.”
Lu Mingze was still going on and on.

It was only rambling and complaining, without any real subject, but his voice sounded so far away. Like a song a wandering bard might sing beside the fire.
“Shut up,” Lu Mingfei said softly.

“You’re the big brother. What you say goes. You tell me to shut up, I shut up.” Lu Mingze shrugged and set the suitcase at Lu Mingfei’s feet. “Stop fussing over the naked girl. She’s ugly now. She isn’t that pretty girl she used to be. Back then, when she was so sexy and sleeping so obediently next door, you weren’t thinking about doing anything with her. What good does holding her so tightly do now? Look at what she left behind. I think some of it was supposed to be shared with you.”

Lu Mingfei laid Erii across his knees and opened the little red suitcase.

She was going so far away. Was this really all she had brought? She was supposed to go to South Korea. She was supposed to start a whole new life there, to wait for someone under a huge flowering crabapple tree with an ice cream in her hand. Would this little bit of luggage have been enough?
The suitcase was packed full. The dresses Lu Mingfei had bought her were folded neatly inside. The shrine maiden’s clothes she used to wear were not there. Besides the gladiator sandals she had worn when she left, there were white shoes with thin straps. Hair ties, hair clips, stockings, and ribbons were packed separately in a plastic bag.

Then there were her precious little toys.

There was something else that took up a lot of room. An album, of all things. These days, photographs were all digital. There were still people who kept albums?

Lu Mingfei opened the thick album. There were no photographs inside. They were postcards.

Travel postcards of Tokyo. Tokyo Skytree, Sensō-ji, Disneyland, Meiji Shrine… Every place Lu Mingfei had taken her was there. He did not know how she had collected them.

He had never wanted to take pictures with her, because he did not want to reveal his identity. So she had collected these postcards to remember the places they had been together.

On the backs were dates and a few simple words.

“04.24. Went to Tokyo Skytree with Sakura. The warmest place in the world is at the top of the Skytree.”

“04.26. Went to Meiji Shrine with Sakura. Someone was having a wedding there.”

“04.25. Went to Disneyland with Sakura. The haunted house was scary. But Sakura was there, so it wasn’t scary.”

They were all silly, sweet little notes like that. The meanings were simple. The wording wasn’t very good. They were just what a girl as blank as a sheet of paper had written after she began to like someone. Every sentence was trying to say “I like someone,” “I like someone,” and “I like someone.”

Her phone was in the suitcase, too. Herzog probably hadn’t thought someone as clueless as her would know how to use a phone. But it was this phone that had given away Erii’s location, and with it, his plan.

On the screen were the mountains of Ehime. Lu Mingfei sat with his back to the camera beside a shrine in the sunset. At some point, she had secretly taken a picture of him.

Lu Mingfei smiled without a sound. He had never known what this felt like.

So everything he did could matter that much in someone else’s world.

So he wasn’t the only one who would look at another person’s back and quietly lose himself in thought.

He took a dress and shoes from the suitcase and began putting them on Erii. Her body was so dried up that the dress slipped on easily. But the shoes and stockings were much harder. Her legs and feet were like withered branches. He had to change her into a dress with a longer skirt, to cover her shriveled body and make her look more like she had when she was alive.

He lifted her in his arms and sat her against the wall of the well. He tidied her hair. Then he put her little toys beside her, one by one. With Rilakkuma, the little yellow chick, Hello Kitty, and the rubber duck keeping her company, perhaps she wouldn’t be afraid.

As he put Rilakkuma down, he happened to turn it over.

He saw the label underneath.

“Sakura & Erii’s Rilakkuma.”

The calm he had struggled so hard to hold on to broke at once. With shaking hands, he turned over every little toy to look underneath.

“Sakura & Erii’s Hello Kitty.”

“Sakura & Erii’s Duck.”

“Sakura & Erii’s Kiiroitori.”

“Sakura & Erii’s Keroro.”

All the labels had been changed. All the toys had been marked as belonging to Sakura and Erii together. The whole world belonged to them together…

This was how big her world was. This was all she had in it. And for the first time, she had shared that world with... someone.

You thought she was a princess, that she had the entire world. But she thought all that she had was you and her little toys.

Lu Mingfei let out a howl like that of a wild beast and stumbled backward, his shoulders violently shaking.

It was a long, long time before he could calm down.
